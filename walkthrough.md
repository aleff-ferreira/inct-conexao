## 🎯 Official Grading Rubric

Each candidate was evaluated strictly against the 4 criteria defined in the official resolution:

1. **Adequação e coerência do Plano de Trabalho com os EET e objetivos específicos do INCT-CONEXÃO (0–60 pts)**:
   Evaluates scientific rigor, methodology, feasibility, and alignment with the 8 Strategic Transversal Stages (EET-1 to EET-8).
2. **Alinhamento do Plano de Atividades à linha de pesquisa e ao(à) orientador(a) pretendido(a) (0–10 pts)**:
   Evaluates fit between proposed activities, supervisor expertise, and host research laboratory within the INCT network.
3. **Histórico Escolar (Coeficiente de Rendimento Acadêmico - CRA) (0–10 pts)**:
   Quantitative grade average from the student's official university transcript.
4. **Currículo Lattes (0–10 pts)**:
   Scientific initiation experience, journal articles, conference papers/posters, teaching assistantships (monitoria), extension projects, and PET involvement.
